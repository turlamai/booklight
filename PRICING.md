# Booklight commercial scope — suspended

**English** · [简体中文](PRICING.zh-CN.md)

## Decision 2026-10-29: file-first reader and editor

Booklight is being rebuilt as a focused PDF/EPUB reader and light PDF editor. Its core scope is opening files in place, native reading, reliable annotations and explicit PDF export. **No paid Booklight edition, Pro research workbench, price, license activation, checkout or trial is approved for implementation or sale.** The previous `2026-10-v2` proposal for a cross-book research Pro tier (including its ¥128 / US$19 target) is superseded, not an active roadmap.

Reading, editing/export, annotations and local data recovery must never be gated behind a speculative entitlement. BYOK AI is optional, and any provider charges are between the user and their chosen provider. No commercial access system is currently implemented for Booklight.

If a new paid feature is ever justified, it needs a separate owner decision, a clear user result, privacy validation, real platform acceptance, honest payment/refund terms and integration tests. A generic decision in Permit/Commerce or old issue text does **not** authorize adding licensing or telemetry here.

For current product requirements see [file-first design](docs/FILE-FIRST-DESIGN.md), [data contract](shared/spec/DATA-FORMAT.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
